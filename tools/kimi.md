# Kimi

**Shape:** model family with coding surfaces
**Made by:** Moonshot AI

## What it is

A model family with coding-capable offerings, usable through Moonshot's own surfaces and
through third-party clients that let you pick a provider. Parts of it are released openly,
which is unusual in this group and matters for anyone who needs to run or inspect what
they depend on.

## The workflow it pushes you toward

Same as any provider-agnostic option: the client decides the shape, the model decides the
correction cost. Being able to separate those two is a useful habit — it stops you
attributing a tool's ergonomics to its intelligence and vice versa.

Where weights are available, a third option opens that none of the closed tools offer:
running the thing yourself, on your own hardware, for work that cannot leave your network.
That is a niche, but for the people in it there is no substitute.

## What transfers when you leave

The whole loop — context, plan, diff, iterate — plus the same discipline of measuring
correction cost on your own real task rather than trusting a leaderboard.

## What will annoy you

Documentation and community material are thinner and less consistently in English than for
labs with more Western market presence, so setup can eat an evening that the equivalent
elsewhere would not.

And as with any provider-agnostic choice, your experience is the client and the model
multiplied together — so when something is bad, you get to guess which half did it. It is
usually the half you did not suspect.
